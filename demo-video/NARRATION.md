# K.I.W.I. demo narration (for HeyGen)

Make one HeyGen clip per block below and save it as `public/voice/<file>` (the file name is under each heading; .mp4 shows
the avatar in the bottom-right corner, .mp3/.wav/.m4a is voice only). Then `npm run render`: each clip starts with its
scene, the music dips under it, and a scene grows if its clip runs longer, with the others giving time back so the video
stays 4:00. Times below are where each scene sits before any clips are added; the captions use the same words.

## [0:00–0:10] Opening

File: `title.mp4`

Meet K.I.W.I. — Knowledge, Intelligence and Workflow Integration. The AI workspace where conversations become decisions — and decisions become action.

## [0:10–0:24] Sign in

File: `signin.mp4`

K.I.W.I. turns what your team says and shares into summaries, action items and answers that cite their sources — in English and Thai. And nothing the AI drafts takes effect until a person confirms it.

## [0:24–0:40] Workspace

File: `workspace.mp4`

Sign in with Google and you land in your team’s workspace. One rail takes you to Chat, Workspace, Calendar and Agents — everything the team needs, in one place.

## [0:40–1:10] Chat

File: `chat.mp4`

In a channel, your team talks in real time — in English or Thai. A message sent offline waits, and arrives the moment you reconnect.

## [1:10–1:17] Start a meeting

File: `meet.mp4`

When it’s time to talk, start a Google Meet straight from the channel.

## [1:17–1:32] The call

File: `call.mp4`

K.I.W.I. follows the call and captures every word of the transcript. No one has to take notes.

## [1:32–1:48] Minutes of meeting

File: `mom.mp4`

When the call ends, the minutes arrive in the channel — the overview, the decisions and the open questions, each linked to the moment it was said.

## [1:48–2:24] Meetings and draft tasks

File: `meetings.mp4`

Every meeting is kept in Meetings, with its full briefing. Alongside it, the AI has already drafted the action items. You review them, assign an owner and accept — and the draft becomes a real task. The AI proposes; your team decides.

## [2:24–2:34] Board

File: `board.mp4`

Confirmed tasks land on the Board, filed by the meeting or channel they came from.

## [2:34–3:07] Ask

File: `ask.mp4`

When you have a question, just ask. K.I.W.I. searches your channels, meetings and files, and answers with citations you can open — in English or Thai. Every AI result carries a reference code and its sources, so you always know where it came from.

## [3:07–3:43] Calendar

File: `calendar.mp4`

The Calendar gives you Month, Week and Day views. Schedule a meeting for your team or the whole organization, and K.I.W.I. creates the event with a Meet link, ready to join.

## [3:43–4:00] Close

File: `close.mp4`

K.I.W.I. runs entirely on Google Cloud, powered by Gemini. Chat, meetings, knowledge and tasks — unified in one AI workspace, where every conversation turns into action. K.I.W.I. — the AI workspace for your team. Thank you.
